## Session record — 2026-08-10, from the transcript

**Stage 1 — The author's opening formulation (as received).** CCC proposed as distinct from the leakage constant (TLC): a one-time "snap" cost of configuration, G-denominated as leakage is C-denominated. Cost proposed as payable over 10 channels (3 faces × 3 dimensions + 1), with the triple named "Mass, Gravity, C"; the electron proposed as cost-bearer; the cost proposed as entering **both** the proton-mass derivation (AP30) and the G derivation (AP28). Explicit standing instruction: work from the axiom outward, never from established physics inward.

**Stage 2 — Assistant analysis (one response; numbers and arguments together).** Corpus read in full (AP06, AP10, AP19, AP24, AP28, AP30, Rosin Ø Proofs, Ø Predictions). Computed in the same response: (a) AP30's proton result stands at +0.008 ppb, inside experimental uncertainty — no room for any visible new term; (b) per-channel ε-compounding on G gives −6.4% — excluded; (c) a channel-scaled cost shifts m_p/m_e by up to eight orders of magnitude above allowance — excluded; (d) a single one-ε cost on the coupling closes the +0.69% overshoot to about −0.036%; (e) the backwards-demanded fraction is ≈0.94–0.95 ε, and the nearby clean fractions 20/21 and 17/18 land closer to the CODATA centre — **computed and fenced in the same response** as data-generated and inadmissible. The 10 reinterpreted, not eliminated: 6 faces + 3 dimensions + 1 Actualization State = the vertex count of the coupling graph whose 21 edges AP28 already proves; explicitly stripped of numerical work. The phrase "3 faces" flagged as colliding with locked vocabulary.

**Honest label for Stage 2, stated by the witness:** the 1×ε magnitude argument (the axiom's single unpaired element) was articulated in the same response in which the corrected value was first computed, against a residual (+0.69%) that was already published canon before the session. The accurate genre is **postdiction with refused tuning**: the data preferred ≈0.95 ε; the paper states 1×ε and armed a switch against every refinement. Readers weigh the argument's content knowing its timing.

**Stage 3 — The author's ruling.** Target confirmed as structural completeness, not numerical agreement ("fuck the numbers"). Author corrected his own opening: per-configuration, not per-channel. Accepted 1×ε as the axiom's arithmetic and the vertex reading of the 10. Commissioned the draft.

**Stage 4 — Draft v0.1.** Six propositions; electron-as-cost-bearer demoted from mechanism; realised prediction computed: **G = 6.6719165 × 10⁻¹¹ (−0.036%)**. This is the first and only computation of the corrected prediction in the record. **The number never moves again** through v0.2, v0.3, or the working paper.

**Stage 5 — Second reviewer, round one (materials supplied by the author).** Findings: an internal contradiction between §4 and §6; the unanswered exponent question (one factor of (1+ε) against ~10⁸⁰ configurations); §5's value graded DERIVED when it is a selection among AP06's open domain family; §7 an extension of AP24's licence, not an application; convention errors in §10's fenced-fraction arithmetic. Resolution adopted: the **arena reading** — one arena, one snap, one ε; matter's commitments already booked as mass via AP29. Draft v0.2 built. **Numerical motion between v0.1 and v0.2: zero.** The step was driven by structural consistency, not by measurement.

**Stage 6 — Working paper WP-CCC.1 (Appendix C, Tier 1).** Seventeen published determinations of G compiled and verified, including NIST-26 (Metrologia 63, 025012, published 2026-04-16 — after every CCC formulation and before this session). Verdict: Candidate 1 (apparatus leakage carries the full 0.69%) strained to near-exclusion by a measured reproducibility limit (strain factor 28); Candidate 3 consistent and not confirmed; the +279 ppm mean residual above the realised value reported open. Retroactive boundary scoring refused; Tier 2 pre-registered blind.

**Stage 7 — Second reviewer, round two.** Corrections adopted after verification: the between-class variance statistic was inflated by singleton classes (proper ANOVA: F(8,8)=2.47, p=0.11 — at best marginal); the anelasticity mechanism is already spent inside the CODATA input values; BIPM-removal relieves the residual by only 15 ppm; HUST-05 reconciled to the current CODATA table. Rev 1.1. **Verdicts unchanged; supports corrected.**

**Stage 8 — Principal reviewer, round on v0.2 + rev 1.1.** Reproduced every statistic; verified NIST-26 independently; endorsed the Tier 1 architecture. Demanded: the full generative trail published (this note); KS-CCC.1 widened from fractions to structural selections; the per-experiment exclusion stated in sigma; a Candidate 0 (null) paragraph; KS-CCC.3 sharpened into a genuine fork. All adopted. One reconstruction corrected by the transcript: the ten was de-numericalised, not withdrawn; and the v0.1 → v0.2 step involved no numerical convergence.

**Stage 9 — This note, AP44 Draft v0.3, WP-CCC.1 rev 1.2.** Author's rulings recorded: the paper is **Artist's Proof 44**; the provenance record is published beside it.
