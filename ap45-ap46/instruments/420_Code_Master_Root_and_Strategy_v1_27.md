# §0 — How to read this document

This document supersedes the prior Audit Strategy v1.24 and Root Document v3.1. How a fresh Claude instance uses this document. Read it end to end before drafting, auditing, compiling, reviewing, or rewriting anything. The work is large, the locks are precise, and the artist who has built it for over thirty years deserves an instance that reads carefully before writing. The document is ordered so a fresh instance gets identity and protocol first, then state, then voice, then locked formulations, then the operational registers (lessons learned, failure modes, standing decisions, open questions), then the work tracker. Nothing is decorative. Every section is referenced from the protocol.

What this document is not. It is not the work itself. It is the index, the spine, the locks, the working memory. The work is on the website. The bound notebooks, the standalone Artist’s Proofs, the Five Doors books, the Ø Models series, the Master Kill Switch Registry — those are the body of work. This document is the structure that lets a fresh instance pick up that body of work and continue without breaking continuity.

Two kinds of strategy document. This Root is the corpus-wide guide — identity, canon, voice, locks, continuity, the state of the whole work. Each book being drafted also has its own bible: the book-specific plan (architecture, source map, build spec). When a book is written, upload this Root and that book’s bible together. # §5 — Voice and register — the three-mode protocol

## The three-mode protocol (from AP01)

Every Artist’s Proof and every Five Doors book uses three modes in proportion, derived from AP01. The proportions vary by register; the modes do not.

Mode 1 — Formal. Definitions, theorems, propositions, proofs, postulates, falsifiers. Precise, sparse, declarative. The mode of the formal apparatus. Used for axiom statements, derivations, kill switches, and any claim that must be falsifiable. Heaviest in the Artist’s Proofs; present in every volume.

Mode 2 — Narrative. The structural argument carried in flowing prose. The mode that bridges between the formal apparatus and the reader. Lands a claim, then turns it from three angles: structural, experiential, practical. The body register of Ø Dissolutions and the Editions register that governs Resolutions, Applications, Horizons.

Mode 3 — Conversational. Direct address. Body-knowable cues. Two jars of water. A grain of sand. The reader’s present-tense reading. The mode that anchors the formal apparatus in something the reader can register with their body. Used sparingly, always to land a structural claim, never to decorate.

The gear-changes between modes are the work’s signature move. A formal block lands; a narrative paragraph bridges; a single conversational sentence anchors. The reader is never lost in the formal apparatus, because Mode 3 returns regularly to body-knowable ground. The reader is never lost in conversation, because Mode 2 reasserts the structure.

## The Editions register (Ø Models, Five Doors)

Calibrated against the newest Ø Dissolutions (not Reader’s Edition; not Root v2.5 sample). The voice that governs every Ø Models volume and every Five Doors book at the body level.

- Direct second-person where it lands. “You are reading this sentence.”

- Long sentences open movements; short sentences land them.

- Concrete physical grounding before any abstraction. Two jars of water. A grain of sand. A fingerprint on a glass.

- One-sentence paragraphs reserved for landing structural claims. “It is forced.” “And the grain is the bit.” Used sparingly; used precisely.

- Lower-case operations, capital structural terms. “dissolution” lowercase; R, S, B, C, AS, Ø capitalised.

- Calm. Direct. Never preaches. No consolation. No softening. The structure does not give a fuck about feelings.

- Period markers, not philosopher names — with explicit exceptions. Names are permitted only when load-bearing.

- British spelling throughout. “Realisation” not “realization.” “Behaviour” not “behavior.” Exception: “Actualization State” stays American.

- “Framework” word count: zero. The work does not use the word.

- “Zero free parameters” always — never “zero fitted parameters.” One measured input: α, the fine-structure constant.

## Forbidden registers

Avoid actively in drafting: academic “we” (use “the chapter” for chapter-level moves, second-person “you” for reader-level moves); strategic-document register (“This chapter argues that…”); mystical phrasing (“the break still breaking”, “the eternal now”); consolation; sycophancy or flattery towards the reader; survey or literature-review register; hedging (“perhaps,” “it might be argued,” “one could say”); contest language (“win,” “winning,” “the win”) — the posture is never adopted, because the one who already won does not play; and “guess” or “wager” for the work’s claims — the grade is Conjectured: the certainty is the author’s, the proof is the owed debt (held, not proved — the grade describes the proof-state, never the mind-state).
