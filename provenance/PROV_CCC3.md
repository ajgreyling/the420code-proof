## 2 — Erratum and correction: the true topology of 10 August

PROV-CCC.2 §3 framed the reviewer conversation as occurring "before the session." The transcript shows something more interesting and more important to record: **two sessions ran in parallel on the same day, opened with the same message and the same eight corpus uploads, and were cross-pollinated by the author** — the production thread's drafts (v0.1, v0.2, the WP bundle) were carried into the reviewer thread as uploads; the reviewer thread's verdicts were carried back as pasted feedback. Clock ordering between the two openings is not established; ordering *within* the day is established by artifact flow (a draft must exist before its review; a review must exist before its adoption).

Two consequences, both favourable to the record's honesty. First, several formulations were killed **twice, independently, the same day** — the per-channel and channel-scaled costs died in the production thread against AP30's precision and in the reviewer thread against the deuteron ladder. Second, **Appendix C was executed twice independently**: the reviewer thread ran it on the sixteen CODATA determinations (its Exchange 7, `Appendix_C_Execution_The_Leakage_Discriminator_v0_1.md`); the production thread ran it on seventeen including NIST-26 (WP-CCC.1). Both converged on the same Candidate 1 verdict by different arguments — an internal one (AP06's own η range) and an external one (the NIST-26 replication bound). An accidental replication of the analysis itself, now on the record.
