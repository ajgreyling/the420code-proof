## 0 — The claim, in one paragraph

Every actualization is a blink: a configuration breaks and re-couples as one singular event — the eye closes and opens — and the event has a cost. The cost is not paid in energy. On the energy face the event carries only the ordinary threshold energetics every break already involves, unchanged, which is why no budget anywhere owes it a line. The cost is paid on the possibility-and-coupling ledger: exactly one grain of distinction — one ε-grain, α-worth, dimensionless — spent as the pruning of the alternatives not taken, entangling the outcome with its environment. There is a cost to turning right, and it is turning left. The world already measures this ledger's outside face and calls it probability. That is the claim; the rest of the paper is its derivation, its grades, its cage of pretty coincidences, and its kill switches.

**Theorem (the toll).** At every actualization, exactly one grain of distinction is spent on the possibility ledger — the alternatives not taken, α-worth, paid by the event as its own occurrence. It exists because ties heal: a break at exact balance is a non-event the record cannot keep, so every break carries a strict win. It is spent because vanished alternatives book nowhere: the toll dies with its payer, accumulates into nothing, and leaves the deuteron wall nothing to bite. And it is one regardless of the crowd — turning right when there were a thousand lefts still spends one grain, because the count is the event's, not the alternatives'.
